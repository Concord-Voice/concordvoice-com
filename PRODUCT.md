# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People seeking a secure, private online third space: a place to gather, talk, watch, chat, and build a shared space without specialist technical knowledge. The initial audience includes privacy-conscious communities, gaming groups, development teams, and organizations; Concord Voice is intended to broaden beyond them.

## Product Purpose

Concord Voice provides voice, video, and chat in a privacy-focused online community space. Success means people can have a full-featured place to be together without trading away the privacy of their conversations.

## Positioning

End-to-end encryption and privacy take priority over productization. Privacy is a product default, not a premium feature or an expert-only setting.

## Operating Context

People use Concord Voice through the cross-platform Electron desktop client and browser-based Admin Portal, in hosted communities or self-hosted deployments. Communities organize servers, channels, and direct messages for text, voice, video, screen sharing, and shared spaces. The public website is a static Astro site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- End-to-end encryption protects messages and voice/video communications.
- Users can use a hosted service or self-host Concord Voice on their own infrastructure.
- The source is available under CVSL 1.0 and converts to AGPL-3.0-or-later on 2030-02-15.
- The product must be accessible, transparent, and feature-rich without making privacy tools difficult to use.
- The public website is privacy-first and uses no third-party trackers.

## Brand Commitments

Concord Voice is a secure, privacy-focused online third space. Product communication must preserve the commitments to privacy, transparency, meaningful user control, and a complete modern communication experience.

## Product Principles

1. Privacy is the baseline, not a trade-off users must discover or configure.
2. A private alternative must still feel complete and easy to use.
3. People and communities should retain meaningful control over their space and infrastructure.
4. Security and privacy claims must be transparent and supportable.
5. Accessibility broadens who can participate in a private online third space.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Concord Voice must be accessible to anyone and everyone; privacy and a full-featured experience must not require specialist knowledge.
