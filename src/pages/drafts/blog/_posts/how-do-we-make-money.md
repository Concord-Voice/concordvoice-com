Let's talk about the elephant in the room. We've spent several blog posts telling you that we don't sell your data, we can't read your messages, and we refuse to run ads. So... how does this business actually work?

Fair question. Here's the whole answer.

## Own Your Voice

We believe in a free and open platform, and as such, **personal use of Concord Voice is free, always and forever.** No matter whether you choose to use a server on the Concord Voice Network or host it yourself, we won't ever charge you for privacy or messaging or limit your communications. We strive to be community over profit.

## So, Why Offer Subscriptions?

We want to be able to provide a few things with subscriptions:

**An enhanced experience for those who want it**, without limiting the general platform for non-subscribers.

*(Marketing calls it "maximizing value," or something like that...)*

**The ability to grow and scale as our user base does**, which means needing more infrastructure for all you beautiful people, and likewise, extremely talented people to keep it all running.

*(Have you ever attempted to run Kubernetes before? It's such a pain in the...)*

**The ability to keep development ongoing** so we can continue innovating, adding new features, improving security, and conducting bug testing, etc.

*(Seriously, the cost of compliance alone is miserable... Who likes lawyers anyway?)*

Wanna know where you'd be putting your money? **Take a look at our **[**About**](/about-us)** page** to understand the people behind the app and its development.

## What's Free and What Isn't

Let's get one thing straight: the core platform doesn't cost a thing. Encryption, voice calls, video calls, messaging, DMs, screen sharing, creating and joining servers ; all free. You don't hit a paywall to talk to your friends. You don't get a 7-day trial that nags you into upgrading. There is no "freemium bait and switch" here. If you use Concord Voice and never spend a dollar, you're still getting a fully encrypted communications platform that most companies would charge you for. That's by design.

What subscriptions unlock are enhancements. Things like studio-grade audio quality, animated profile customization, bigger file uploads (256 MB for Supersonic), deeper message search, and a server-wide quality lift for every member of your boosted server ; the exact ceiling depends on the Mach tier. Nice-to-haves that make the experience richer if you want them, but the platform is complete without them.

## How the Tiers Work

We have a tier system themed around breaking the sound barrier, because, well, it's a voice app. Sue us *(please don’t actually...)*

For individuals, the free tier is called **Sonic**. Everything you need, nothing held back ; encryption, voice and video calls, messaging, DMs, and creating or joining servers. Above that, **Supersonic** is $8.99/month (go yearly and a month's free). It's the "just give me everything" tier: full customization, hi-fi audio, 256 MB file uploads, deeper message search, and opt-in early access to new features ; all in one subscription. The key benefit is portability: your Supersonic uplift travels with you to every server you join, boosted or not. No five-rung pricing ladder where the feature you actually want is always one tier above the one you bought.

On the server side, every hosted server starts free with a tier called **Groundspeed** ; unlimited channels, E2EE, standard voice and video, screen sharing. For communities that want more, the **Mach** tiers unlock the server for everyone in it: custom emoji and stickers, an animated server banner, better file limits, a shared storage pool, and the big one ; unlocked audio and video quality for all members on the server, not just the person who paid. The server owner sets the ceiling, and everyone benefits. There are three Mach tiers, each a-la-carte: **Mach 1** at $4.99/month (1080p60 video, up to 510 kbps audio, 128 MB files, 250 emoji/75 stickers/30 soundboards, 250 GB storage, animated server banner); **Mach 2** at $14.99/month (1440p60, custom invite links, 256 MB files, 350 emoji/100 stickers/40 soundboards, 1 TB storage, server tag); and **Mach 3** at $34.99/month (4K60, 512 MB files, 500 emoji/150 stickers/55 soundboards, 2.5 TB storage). Every Mach tier lifts the whole server ; audio is the standard the channel admin sets, applying to every member including free ones, and video tracks the better of your personal cap or the server floor.

**Hypersonic** bundles a Supersonic account and a Mach 2 server together at $21.99/month. It exists because we noticed that the most active people on the platform are usually both members of communities and running their own. Two separate bills for that is annoying, so we combined them and knocked a few bucks off.

And for groups, a few plans share premium instead of buying it one seat at a time. **Wing** is two Supersonic seats ; ideal for a pair of friends or partners who both want the personal uplift. **Squadron** scales that to a whole team: one Captain, one bill, Supersonic for every member plus one Mach boost (Captain picks the tier; Mach 2 by default), priced well under buying each seat à la carte; extra servers can be added a-la-carte at the ladder price. And **Afterburner** is a fair pledge-to-unlock pool ; nobody is charged until total pledges reach the tier price, so a community can collectively crowd-fund a Mach 2 or Mach 3 boost, with about $5 per share as a practical minimum. Seat counts and per-plan pricing live on the pricing page.

For full pricing details, feature lists, and all the fine print about hardware requirements and disclaimers ; that's on our [pricing](/pricing) page. This blog post is about the philosophy, not the spreadsheet.

## Self-Hosting: Free, for Real

If you host Concord Voice on your own hardware for personal or non-commercial use, every feature unlocked, hardware-limited (you run your own media plane). Not "some features." Not "basic tier." Everything. The stuff that Supersonic and Mach users pay for on our hosted platform is included out of the box when you run it yourself. Your costs are your infrastructure ; we don't charge you for software features on hardware we're not paying for.

If you want to connect your self-hosted server to the Concord Voice Network and get official support, that's an add-on we call **Ground Control** ; $120/year, with multi-year discounts if you commit longer. Without it, you're fully independent: no support, no network sync, no relationship with us whatsoever. That's fine. That's the point of self-hosting.

Commercial use requires a CVSL commercial license, which is a conversation, not a surprise invoice.

## Organizations: Enterprise and OEM

Two very different kinds of organizations end up looking at Concord Voice, and we built paths for both.

The first is the organization that needs to **own its communications stack**. Government agencies, law firms, healthcare systems, financial institutions, and companies with data residency requirements or air-gapped networks. For these teams, "trust us, it's encrypted" doesn't cut it ; they need the entire deployment on their infrastructure, under their control, with source code access for audit. That's our **Enterprise** path. We're also building LDAP/SAML SSO integration and compliance audit tooling, because if you're in a regulated industry, you need that stuff, and we know it.

The second is the organization that wants to **sell Concord Voice as part of their own offering**. Managed service providers, hosting companies, technology partners, resellers. That's our **MSP & OEM** path ; wholesale fleet licensing starting at ten servers, 30–40% off retail, with optional white-label branding. You handle customer relationships and frontline support; we handle the deep technical work behind the scenes. Your brand or ours, your pricing, your customers.

Both paths get self-hosted deployment, E2EE, source code audit access, and dedicated onboarding. The difference is in the licensing model and support structure. Full details and a comparison table are on our Organizations page.

## How We Compare

We're not going to pretend we exist in a vacuum. Here's where we sit relative to the industry, honestly:

Our individual pricing lands at or slightly below the $6–$10/month range that's standard for voice and community platforms ; except ours includes end-to-end encrypted messaging as a baseline, which most competitors either don't offer or charge extra for.

Our server pricing starts in line with the $20–$35/month range for community boost-style models, and quality unlocks apply to every member in the server, not just the subscriber.

Hypersonic at $21.99/month replaces what would typically cost $40–$50/month if you could even assemble a comparable bundle (a personal account plus a server tier) from competing platforms. Most don't offer one.

Self-hosting is free, whereas enterprise self-hosted licensing elsewhere costs thousands per year. Ground Control at $120/year is, as far as we can tell, the first flat-rate network and support offering for individual self-hosters that doesn't require an enterprise budget.

We publish a full comparison table with specific numbers on our pricing page. We'd rather you verify our claims than take our word for it.

## The Bottom Line

We make money by offering a better experience to people who want it, not by exploiting the people who don't. The free tier isn't a trap. The paid tiers aren't ransom for features that should be free. And the organizational paths exist because we'd rather build infrastructure for the people who need it than chase ad revenue from the people who don't.

No dark patterns. No shareholders. No ads. No data sales. Just subscriptions, honestly priced, transparently explained, for people who want to support what we're building or need more from it.
